Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. PutPlot has no account, no cloud, and no tracking.

How do I lock my list?
PutPlot uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. You can toggle this in Settings.

I forgot where I saved a backup / lost my phone.
Because data is stored only on your device, it can't be recovered if the app is deleted or the device is reset. We recommend keeping a device backup.
