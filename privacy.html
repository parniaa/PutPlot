PutPlot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

PutPlot is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. PutPlot does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. We have no servers, no analytics, and no tracking.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter, item names, locations, and photos, is stored only on your device. Your data never leaves your iPhone unless you choose to include it in your own device or iCloud backup, which is managed by Apple and controlled by you.

Camera and photo access

PutPlot asks for camera and photo library access only so you can attach an optional photo to an item. Photos you add are saved on your device and are never uploaded anywhere by us.

Authentication

PutPlot can lock your list using Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. This authentication is handled entirely by iOS. PutPlot never sees or stores your biometric data or passcode.

Third parties

PutPlot does not use any third-party services, advertising, or software development kits that collect data.

Children's privacy

PutPlot does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
